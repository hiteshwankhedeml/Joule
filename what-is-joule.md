# 🟢 What is Joule

* It goes <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**beyond answering questions**</mark>&#x20;
* <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**It executes work, coordinates agents**</mark>, and acts within the processes and data that your business runs on.
* You express your goals or intent, and Joule brings together the right data, insights, workflows, and agents to help achieve those outcomes, across SAP applications and relevant third-party systems.
* Alongside Joule Work, <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Joule Desktop is a separate standalone solution**</mark> in the Joule stack

<mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Foundations:**</mark>

* <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Deep process and industry knowledge:**</mark>
  * Joule agents <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**understand the sequence, rules and implication of business processes, not just the data those processes produce**</mark>
* <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Semantically rich business data:**</mark>
  * <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">SAP's data model carries business meaning, not just values</mark>
  * This contextual understanding is what makes agent decisions reliable rather than technically correct but operationally wrong
* <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Enterprise-grade governance:**</mark>
  * Actions taken by agents are governed, auditable, and reversible
  * Provides a <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**complete audit trail**</mark>
  * <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Data is never used to train third-party models**</mark>
