# Analytical Insights with SAP Analytics Cloud

* In an analytical conversation, Joule can provide users with key metrics and charts as answers to their business  &#x20;questions.
* If sales data is made available to the analytical insights feature, you can ask questions relating  &#x20;to it
* It can show data using graphs, bars etc
* We can also ask how we want it to be displayed



**Examples:**

* Net revenue and gross margin for each product
* What is the discount for «product xy»
* Gross margin from 2022 to 2025
